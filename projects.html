Undergraduate & Personal Projects

Undergraduate Research: Developing a Decision Support System for Pharmaceutical Production Planning Using Linear Programming (2026)

This research focused on pharmaceutical production planning under fixed batch sizes, fluctuating monthly demand, and GMP-related production constraints. A Linear Programming (LP) optimization model was developed to maximize batch production while considering raw material availability and batch-level material requirements.

The model was initially programmed and validated using MATLAB and later transformed into a user-friendly web-based Decision Support System (DSS) using Streamlit in Python. The developed system supports multiple rounding approaches for LP results and enables real-time checking of raw material availability corresponding to production decisions. The DSS significantly reduced excess production and shortages compared to manual planning methods while supporting balanced and inventory-conscious pharmaceutical production planning.

Tools: Python (Streamlit Dashboard), MATLAB

Group Project: Optimal Control of Bear Populations in Protected and Harvested Regions: A Metapopulation Approach (2026)

This project developed an optimal control strategy for managing black bear populations in a metapopulation system consisting of protected park areas and surrounding harvested forest regions. Using Pontryagin’s Maximum Principle, an optimal control problem was formulated and solved to minimize human-bear conflicts while maintaining sustainable population levels. The model incorporated density-dependent migration patterns and analyzed the effects of different border configurations between protected and harvested areas. Numerical solutions were obtained using a forward-backward sweep method implemented in MATLAB.

Tools: MATLAB

Group Project: Public Transit Timetable Optimization (2026)

This project demonstrated the application of Answer Set Programming (ASP) for solving complex constrained optimization problems related to public transportation scheduling. By declaratively modeling transit network structures, demand patterns, and operational rules, a concise ASP encoding was developed for timetable optimization. Clingo efficiently generated optimal timetables that satisfied operational constraints while improving service quality and scheduling efficiency.

Tools: Clingo

Green Myopia in Organic Cotton Products (2024)

This project focused on evaluating customer awareness, perceptions, and purchasing behavior related to organic cotton products and green marketing practices. Market research was conducted using structured questionnaires to understand how sustainability claims influence consumer decision-making and customer attitudes. The study identified the existence of green myopia in marketing strategies, where excessive focus on sustainability may reduce customer attraction and product appeal.

Tools: Excel

Modeling Mechanical Vibrations (2024)

Simulated mechanical vibration systems using MATLAB to analyze dynamic behavior under various forcing and damping conditions. The project involved developing mathematical models of single-degree-of-freedom and multi-degree-of-freedom systems, solving equations of motion numerically, and visualizing displacement, velocity, and acceleration responses. Key analyses included natural frequency determination, resonance effects, and the influence of damping ratios on system stability. The simulations helped in understanding real-world vibration problems such as vehicle suspension dynamics and structural oscillations.

Tools: MATLAB
